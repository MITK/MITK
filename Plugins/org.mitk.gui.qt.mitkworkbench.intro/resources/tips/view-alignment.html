Aligning views to an image

An image is a grid of discrete voxels. The views, however, are not tied to any particular image: after loading, they span all data along the standard anatomical directions. Their slices then do not necessarily match the voxel layers of an image, for example because the image is tilted (top) or because several images with different voxel grids are loaded (bottom).

That is fine for viewing, but some plugins work on the individual voxels of one particular image. They need views that lock onto the voxel grid of that image, so that every slice matches exactly one layer of its voxels.

To align the views to an image, right-click it in the Data Manager and choose Fit views to selection. Some plugins detect views that are not aligned and prominently present an Align views button.
